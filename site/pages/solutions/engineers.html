How the proof runs

The exact identities, simulated inside a disposable database.

The lab applies your migrations to a local Postgres with Supabase’s roles, auth.uid() and default grants, seeds fake rows for two users, then sets the role and JWT claims for each identity.

  • Never connects to your project; we never need a password or service key
  • The smallest model writes first; spend capped per check
  • A fix that fails any identity goes to a person, never shipped as proven

How proof works

verify a receipt · python, simplified
r = get(f"{API}/receipts/{check_id}")
key = get(f"{API}/.well-known/patchlabs-receipt-key")
msg = json.dumps(r["receipt"], sort_keys=True,
                 separators=(",", ":")).encode()
Ed25519PublicKey.from_public_bytes(
    b64decode(key["public_key"])).verify(
    b64decode(r["signature"]), msg)

Questions

Engineer questions.

Something not covered? Email the founder.

What does the lab not model?

Anything outside your migrations: changes made by hand in the dashboard, extensions or settings not in the repo, and backends other than Supabase/Postgres. The report lists what it couldn’t rebuild.

Does it understand intentionally public tables?

Mark them as intended once; later checks keep reporting them but stop alerting. Decisions go into the signed receipt.

Can I see the raw findings?

Yes. Every finding has the same JSON shape across the report, API and webhooks: ID, severity, object, file, line, evidence, proof and fix.

Is this a pentest?

No. It tests specific, documented things in depth. Use it alongside a pentest for a high-stakes launch.
