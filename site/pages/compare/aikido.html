PatchLabs vs Aikido

Aikido covers your whole stack. PatchLabs goes deep on who can read your users’ data.

Aikido is a broad security platform: code, cloud, pentesting and runtime protection, with a connector inside Lovable. PatchLabs does one job. It rebuilds your database from your migrations, finds the rows strangers can read, writes the fix and proves it before your engineer approves it.

Last checked 10 Nov 2026Sources at the endIndependent of Aikido

Choose Aikido when

You need one platform for everything a security team watches.

  • You want dependencies, static analysis, secrets, cloud, containers and runtime protection in one place
  • You need a test of your live app, including auth bypass and cross-tenant access, with a shareable report
  • You work across many repos and languages and want issues synced to Jira or Linear
  • You need a vendor with security certifications for your own compliance work

Choose PatchLabs when

Your biggest risk is a Supabase database leaking personal data.

  • You want each fix proven on a rebuilt copy of your database, as anon, stranger and owner, before you see it
  • You’d rather nothing touched your live app, cloud account or real data
  • You want a reviewable migration and a plain-English answer to “should this table be public?”
  • You want a free, focused check today, with published prices and our own cost per check

Where we’re different

The proof happens before the fix reaches you, not after it ships.

We rebuild the database from your migrations in a disposable Postgres with Supabase’s roles and grants, seed fake rows, and run the same read as each person. A fix that fails is never called proven; it goes to a person.

  • No access to your live app, cloud or database
  • Smallest Claude model first, with a spend cap per check
  • Median model cost $0.46 per app (n=20, 9 Nov 2026)

Questions

Asked about Aikido.

Can I use both?

Yes, and if you need broad coverage you should. Aikido covers dependencies, code, cloud and live testing. PatchLabs adds a proven fix for the specific question of who can read your database rows, and re-checks it on a schedule.

Is PatchLabs a cheaper Aikido?

No. We do much less. If you need dependency scanning, cloud checks or a pentest, we don’t replace Aikido. We go deeper on one risk that’s common in AI-built apps: in our study, 106 of 318 rebuilt apps let the wrong people read personal data (PatchLabs lab study, Nov 2026).

Aikido is inside Lovable. Why isn’t PatchLabs?

We work from the code in GitHub, so we also cover apps exported from Lovable or built with Bolt, Replit and Cursor. We have no partnership with Lovable.

Does PatchLabs have SOC 2 or ISO 27001?

No, and we won’t imply otherwise. Our security page says exactly what we access, what we keep and where it runs.
