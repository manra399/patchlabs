Questions

Straight answers.

Something not covered? Email the founder and you’ll get a reply from the person who built it.

Isn’t Lovable’s or Supabase’s built-in check enough?

Use them: they are good at flagging common misconfigurations. They tell you a rule looks risky. They don’t rebuild your database and test, as each kind of user, whether a fix actually works. That proof is what we add. Detailed comparisons.

Do I need all four product lines?

You get them all. Every check runs Lab and Code together, every finding goes through Fix, and Watch keeps re-checking. They are one product with four parts, on every plan including Free.

Do you touch my live database?

No. We work only from your GitHub repo and rebuild the database in a disposable lab with fake rows. Checks never connect to your live app or database, and the fix reaches production only when your engineer applies it. The one exception is opt-in: Live posture loads your public home page once a day, like any visitor, and only on a domain you verify.

What if the fix can’t be proven?

Then we don’t call it proven. The report says a person is reviewing it, and explains why. In our lab study 2 of 20 fixes went to a person. When a fix depends on a business choice, such as whether sellers’ emails should be public, the report asks you in plain English with a recommended answer.

Who approves the change?

You or your engineer. The fix is a migration file you download, or commit to your repo on GitHub in one click. Nothing changes until someone applies it. Automatic pull requests need our GitHub App, which is coming.

Is this a pentest or a compliance certificate?

Neither. A pentest is a person attacking your whole system within an agreed scope, and we don’t certify compliance. We test specific, documented things in depth, each with a finding ID, and say plainly what a report couldn’t check. For a high-stakes launch, get a pentest too.

Which apps does it work with?

Public GitHub repos, most often with Supabase migrations in supabase/migrations: most Lovable, Bolt and Supabase projects, and many Replit and Cursor ones. Code checks also read Firebase rules files. Private repos come with our GitHub App.
