What counts

Three things are limited. Fixes aren’t one of them.

Limits reset on the first of each calendar month. Here is exactly what each one means.

apps ▮▮▯ 2 of 3

Apps

A GitHub repo you monitor in your workspace. It stays verified once you’ve proved it’s yours, and keeps its full check history. The same repo can’t be added twice to one workspace.

runs 12 of 30 · scheduled: free

Manual runs

Checks you start yourself, with “Run now” or through the API. Scheduled re-checks are never counted, and they keep running even if you use up your manual runs.

seats ▮▯ 1 of 2

Seats

People in the workspace, including pending invites. Everyone gets the email alerts and can approve fixes or mark a table as meant to be public.

NEVER COUNTED

A fix that fails proof never counts against your limit.We don’t meter fixes at all. A fix that can’t pass all three checks is never called proven: it goes to a person, and the report says why.

Billing questions

Straight answers about money.

Something else? Email the founder. You’ll get a reply from the person who sets the prices.

How do I pay for a paid plan today?

Request the plan in Settings. We set it on your workspace and email you an invoice. Card payments aren’t live yet; when they are, you’ll be able to switch to them.

What happens when I reach a limit?

We never cut coverage mid-month. If you use all your manual runs, “Run now” and API runs are paused until the 1st, but scheduled re-checks keep running and alerts keep arriving. If you reach your app limit, the apps you already have stay monitored; you just can’t add another until you upgrade or remove one.

Do fixes that fail proof count against my plan?

No. We don’t count fixes at all, proven or not. A fix that fails any of the three checks is never called proven: it goes to a person and the report explains why. What counts is apps, manual runs and seats.

Is there a free trial?

Free is the trial, and it isn’t time-limited: one app, every finding, every fix proven, monthly re-checks. Upgrade when you want more apps or weekly re-checks.

How does annual billing work?

Pay for 10 months and get 12: Maker is £190 a year and Agency is £990 a year, with extra Agency apps at £80 a year each. Say “annual” in the note when you request access.

How do I cancel?

Email us. We move you back to Free at the end of the period you’ve paid for. Your reports and check history stay; there’s nothing to cancel on a card because nothing is charged automatically.

Which currency do you bill in? Is there tax?

We bill in pounds sterling. The dollar amounts on this page are approximate, at about $1.30 to the pound, and only for guidance. Prices are shown without tax; if tax applies, your invoice shows it before you pay.

Do you offer discounts for open source or non-profits?

We don’t publish one yet. If you maintain an open-source project or run a non-profit, email us and tell us about it.

Is there a guarantee or a refund promise?

No. We don’t claim any app is secure, and we don’t sell guarantees. What you get is evidence you can check yourself: the exact queries we ran as each identity, their results, and a signed receipt.
