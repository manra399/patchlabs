Products

Four product lines, one report

LabRebuilds your database from its migrations and reproduces each leak as anon, stranger and owner. CodeReads the repo: keys in code and history, vulnerable dependencies, risky AI-written patterns. FixWrites the migration, proves it as three identities, then waits for your engineer to approve. WatchScheduled re-checks, diffs, alerts, a public badge and signed receipts for every result. Live posture part of WatchA daily grade for HTTPS, the certificate, security headers and cookies on the domain you verify. Never touches your database.
