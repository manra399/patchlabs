Privacy

Last updated 9 November 2026.

  • What we collect. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or trackers. If you email us, we keep your email address, your message and the repository link you send, so we can run your leak check and reply.
  • Your code. We read your repository with the access you give us, rebuild your database in our lab using fake data, and never connect to your live app or read your customers’ data.

  • How long. We delete your lab copy within 30 days of the check, and your emails on request.
  • Your rights. Email manraj@patchlabs.dev to see, correct or delete what we hold about you.
