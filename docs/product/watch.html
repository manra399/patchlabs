How it works

Schedule, compare, alert, share.

  1. Schedule

    Weekly or monthly, by plan. Run one by hand, or from CI, any time.

    schedule: weekly next check in 7 days

  2. Compare

    Each finished check is compared with the one before: new, fixed, still leaking, marked as intended.

    new 1 · fixed 0 still 0 · intended 0

  3. Alert

    Email every member when a new leak appears, and POST a signed webhook that works as a Slack incoming webhook.

    X-PatchLabs-Signature v1=…

  4. Share

    An opt-in badge and public proof page, and an Ed25519-signed receipt for every finished check.

    receipt · Ed25519 verify in browser

Badge & proof page

A dated, public record of your last check.

The badge shows the result of your last finished check and when it ran. It links to a proof page that says what was tested and what isn’t covered. It never hides a leak: it stays amber until a re-check comes back clean.

A badge is not a certification. It reports our last check of the access rules and code in your repo.

Live posture

Your live site’s HTTPS and headers, graded daily.

Verify your app’s domain with a file or a meta tag, and Watch also loads your public home page once a day, like any visitor, to grade HTTPS, the certificate, security headers, cookie flags and mixed content. Your team hears about it when the grade drops.

PatchLabs never touches your database. Live posture only loads your public home page, like any visitor, and only on domains you verify.

How Live posture worksTry the free headers check
  • Ten checks, PL-027 to PL-036, each with the fix for Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare and Lovable
  • Re-checked daily while the app’s re-checks are on, or by hand
  • Email and a posture.changed webhook when the grade drops or a finding appears

What Watch can’t see. Re-checks read your repo, not your live database, so a change made by hand in the Supabase dashboard won’t show up until it’s in a migration. Live posture reads only the headers and settings of your public home page. Monitoring isn’t uptime or runtime protection, and a badge isn’t a certification.

Questions

Questions about this product line.

Something not covered? Email the founder.

Do scheduled re-checks use my monthly runs?

No. Scheduled re-checks are never counted, and keep running even if you use all your manual runs.

Will I get an alert every week?

Only when something new appears. Every member gets an email per finished check, and the subject says “new leak” only when there is one. Tables your team marked as intended don’t raise alerts.

Can anyone verify a receipt?

Yes. Receipts are signed with Ed25519 and the public key is published at /.well-known/patchlabs-receipt-key. The proof page verifies them in your browser.

Is the badge public by default?

No. It’s off until an owner turns it on for an app. A private app’s badge shows only “private”.
