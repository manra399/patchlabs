Products

Four product lines, one report.

Lab and Code find the problem, Fix writes a change that has been tested and waits for your engineer to approve it, and Watch keeps checking. Every plan includes all four, free in early access for the first 1,000 users, approved fixes included.

Product lines

LAB · 8 CHECKS

Lab

Rebuilds your database from its migrations in a throwaway copy and reproduces each leak as an anonymous visitor, a signed-in stranger and the owner. Never connects to your live database.

PL-001 to PL-008See Lab
CODE · 13 CHECKS

Code

Reads your repo and its full history for exposed keys, dependencies with known vulnerabilities and the risky patterns AI-written code gets wrong, each with the file and line.

PL-009 to PL-026See Code
FIX · YOUR ENGINEER APPROVES

Fix

Writes the migration, proves it as the same three people, then waits. Nothing reaches your repo until your own engineer approves, and every result comes with a signed receipt.

proven before you see itSee Fix
WATCH · RE-CHECKS AND ALERTS

Watch

Re-checks your app on a schedule, alerts your team only when something new appears, and keeps a dated badge, proof page and signed receipts.

alerts only on changeSee Watch
PART OF WATCH · 10 CHECKS

Live posture

Loads your public home page once a day, like any visitor, on a domain you verify, and grades HTTPS, the certificate, security headers and cookies. Never touches your database.

PL-027 to PL-036See Live posture
