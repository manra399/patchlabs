Honest limits

What PatchLabs doesn’t do.

If you need any of these, use one of the tools on these pages alongside us. We will say so on our own pages before you find out the hard way.

  • Pentests, live-URL scanning or dynamic testing of your running app
  • Dependency or CVE scanning, static analysis of your whole codebase
  • Compliance certification or audit reports
  • Databases other than Supabase/Postgres, for now
  • Private repos and automatic pull requests: coming with our GitHub App

Method

Where these facts come from.

In November 2026 we reviewed the public websites, documentation and pricing pages of 60 security products, from platform built-ins to enterprise security suites. The named pages use only what those companies say about themselves, quoted sparingly and dated. Where a company’s page didn’t state something, we say “not described” rather than guess.

Products change quickly. Each page shows the date we last checked it. If you work at one of these companies and something is out of date, email manraj@patchlabs.dev and we’ll correct it.
