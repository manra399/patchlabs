Questions

Founder questions.

Something not covered? Email the founder.

I’m not technical. Will I understand the report?

Yes. It’s written in plain English: what leaked, who could read it, the fix and what it changes. The SQL is there for whoever applies it.

Is it really free?

Your first app is free, with monthly re-checks, every finding and every fix proven. It isn’t a trial. Maker is £19 a month for 3 apps and weekly re-checks.

Can it break my app?

Nothing changes until you apply the fix. Each fix is proven so the owner still reads their own data, and the report rates the risk that something behaves differently.

What does it not cover?

It checks specific, documented things (each with an ID) in your repo. It isn’t a pentest or a compliance certificate, and it can’t see changes made by hand in your Supabase dashboard.
