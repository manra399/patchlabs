Which one?

From a quick look to a proven fix.

The free tools tell you where to look. Only the lab tells you what a stranger can actually read, and hands you a fix that has been tested.

01Look

Free RLS check

Seconds. Reads your SQL as text.

  • Risky rules, with file and line
  • Can’t tell intended from leaking
  • No fix

Or grade your site’s HTTPS and headers with the headers check.

02Weigh

Leak cost calculator

Minutes. Your own costs, added up.

  • What telling people would cost
  • Who to report to in the UK
  • Doesn’t look at your app

03Prove

Full lab check

About 3.5 minutes (median, lab study, n=20). Free in early access, fixes included.

  • Rebuilds your database from its migrations
  • Reads as anon, stranger and owner
  • Writes the fix and proves it
  • Scans code and git history for keys
