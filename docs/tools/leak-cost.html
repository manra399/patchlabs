The maths

formula
notify   = people × cost to notify one person
support  = people × share who contact you × cost per contact
goodwill = people × credit per person
engineer = hours × cost per hour
total    = notify + support + goodwill + engineer
         + legal advice + anything else

If it happens in the UK

The 72-hour rule.

Under UK GDPR, you must report certain personal data breaches to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) within 72 hours of becoming aware of them.

Which breaches, and what to do. The duty applies when a breach is likely to put people’s rights and freedoms at risk. If the risk is high, you must usually tell the affected people too, without undue delay. The ICO has a self-assessment and the reporting form.

Report a breach on ico.org.uk · ico.org.uk

  • Write down what you know and when you found out. The clock starts when you become aware.
  • Close the leak first. Then work out which records were readable, and by whom.
  • Outside the UK, check your own regulator’s rules: deadlines and thresholds differ.
  • This page is general information, not legal advice. For a real incident, talk to a lawyer or your data protection officer.
